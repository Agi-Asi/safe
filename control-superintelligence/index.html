Paths to control

Six ways researchers hope to stay in charge.

Alignment

Make the AI actually want what we want: RLHF, constitutional training, scalable oversight.

AI Control

Assume it might be misaligned; monitor, sandbox and limit it so it can't cause harm anyway. Redwood Research's agenda.

Interpretability

Read the model's internals to catch deception. Mechanistic interpretability, Neel Nanda's field.

Evaluations

Test for dangerous capabilities, scheming and shutdown resistance before release: METR, Apollo, Palisade.

Corrigibility

Build systems that accept correction and shutdown. MIRI's CAST proposal by Max Harms.

Governance & pacing

Compute tracking, international agreements, "pacing the frontier" so understanding keeps up.
