Today in 60 seconds

DeepSeek open-sourced the engine behind V4.1-Flash, while Anthropic's leaked draft S-1 showed $16.3B in revenue and an $80.7B loss

  • DeepSeek open-sourced DeepInfer (Apache 2.0), the inference engine that serves DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash in production. It runs on NVIDIA SM100 and Huawei Ascend 950, but it's offline inference only: the KV-cache system and load balancing aren't included. Most-liked AI post of the day (~35.6K likes). Verified by TechTarget, The New Stack and others.
  • Anthropic's draft IPO filing (WSJ): H1 2026 revenue of $16.3B (vs $1.1B a year earlier) and an $80.7B net loss, $72B of it non-cash convertible-note charges. Up to $750B in future compute spending. Bloomberg separately reports talks on a second ~$45B Nscale compute deal (not final). Verified.
  • New open-weight models and speed-ups: Ornith-1.5-122B MoE (claims frontier-level and fast on a single DGX, free for 48h on Nous' Hermes Agent, SGLang day-0); EAGLE-4 speculative decoding (claims 961.6 tok/s on Qwen3.8-27B on one B300). Both are the makers' own numbers.
  • Voice and audio stack: ElevenLabs Eleven Realtime v1 Mini/Max (end-to-end voice agents), Mistral Voxtral 3 (STT + TTS + voice agent SDK, 50+ languages, self-hostable), Gradium Phonon (open TTS under 50M params, Apache 2.0, runs on-device), Underdog Bark 3.0 (163 MB).
  • For AI filmmakers: FastH3 Omni was open-sourced (distilled MiniMax H3, 5–15 s text-to-video with audio, up to 9 references). Krea 3 hit #4 on Artificial Analysis image editing, Bria Fibo 2.0 (8B, licensed data) tops AA-Image-Editing v2.1, and earshot v0.3 adds 201 code-made sounds plus frame-accurate SFX timing.
  • Agent and dev tools: Anthropic's OSS Scanner gives critical open-source projects free periodic vulnerability scans with PoCs and patches. OpenAI added 12-hour prompt caching for persistent agents. Beacon (open source, $5.5M seed) shares memory across Claude Code, Cursor and Codex. tsrs is a Rust port of the TypeScript compiler, claimed up to 8x faster.
  • Money: Daytona (agent sandboxes) raised $60M Series B at a $1B valuation, Eka Robotics raised $150M at $1.45B, an agents-web-API startup raised $9M, and OpenAI bought Orchestra Bio. Policy: DHS ordered US civilian agencies to remove DeepSeek products.
  • Hype filter: 'Odisha founder beats GPT-6 Astra', 'Neuralink cures blindness in 6–12 months', 'ChatGPT secretly profiles you' and the many 'Opus 5.5 replaced After Effects / made me $50K' threads are unverified engagement bait. Treat them as noise.
