# The Guardians of AI — Who's Who of AI Safety & Alignment

**97 researchers · 383 verbatim sourced quotes · 336 interviews**

Curated by **AI AGI Engineer**. Roles verified October 2026.

**Live site:** https://agi-asi.github.io/safe/ai-safety-researchers/

An interactive directory of the people shaping AI safety and alignment: frontier-lab safety teams, academic alignment researchers, independent orgs, interpretability, governance & policy, and the pioneers and public voices. Each profile has a bio, key ideas and works, verbatim quotes with sources, interviews, and links.

## Files

- `index.html` — the site (profiles open at `#slug`)
- `data-1.js` … `data-6.js` — site data, loaded as `window.__SD` chunks
- `site-data.json` — the same site data as one JSON file
- `data/all.json` — full research dataset (97 records)
- `*.jpg` — hero and researcher photos
